    Episode 1: Sam

    Orders 1–77
    0/0
    1. Order 01Return home with Lou
      ObjectiveReturn to Sam and Lou's home by walking along the mountain ridges.
      TipsThis is the tutorial for the game's movement, camera and tool controls. When it comes to getting to Sam and Lou's home, it's best to stay atop the mountain ridges as it will help prevent Sam from falling. Make sure you stand still during the gate quake, which occurs when you're roughly 800 metres from their home, and use the targeting system to find safe ground.
      UnlocksN/A

      We've taken a look at what happens if you select the 'I won't do it' choice.

    2. Order 02Deliver the Chiral Network Gateway to C1
      ObjectiveDeliver the Chiral Network, weighs 35 kg, to the C1 city south of Sam and Lou's home. Involves passing a bandit camp and a river.
      TipsThe bandit camp can be avoided by walking south-east around it. Though we still recommend using stealth to avoid being spotted! It's best to take the broken side of the bridge as you can make your way across by climbing from one car to another. A sandstorm will roll in, but you can stay on target by following your route or by using L1 to keep your target on C1. The final river can be swam across or you can use a ladder.
      Unlocks252 Likes.
    3. Order 03Deliver one or more Remote Medical Units to Villa Libre
      ObjectiveDeliver at least one Remote Medical Unit, weighs 20kg (40kg if taking two), to Villa Libre. Located north-west of C1. Involves travelling past bandit camp to the west and deep river to the east. Timefall, corrosive rain, will fall.
      TipsIt's worth bringing Container Repair Spray with you to repair your cargo from the corrosive effects of Timefall. If you want to avoid the bandit camp, be prepared to cross the deep river twice. Going through the bandit camp will most likely involve combat if you're not lucky with stealth. Avoiding both involves travelling through mountainous terrain. Whatever route you take, it's a good idea to stop by the Postbox for extra supplies and, if you're playing online, benefit from the supplies left by other players.
      Unlocks262 Likes as base reward, with 100 Additional Likes for delivering two containers and having average contents damage 5% or less.
    4. Order 04Recover all Chiral Density Gauges stolen by bandits
      ObjectiveRetrieve the two stolen Chiral Density Gauges from the bandit camp and take them to the C1 South Distribution Center.
      TipsYou'll most likely have to engage in some combat here, so bring weapons, Blood Bags and at least one PCC. If you prefer stealth, then it's best to wait till night falls and build a Watchtower so you can tag any enemy from a distance. It's best to head directly south from Villa Libre to the bandit camp. Make sure you run away from any of the spider monsters if they start chasing Sam!
      UnlocksUnlocks Blood Grenade Lvl 1 and Timefall Shelter. 242 Likes as base reward, with 320 Additional Likes for average container damage 5% or less.

      We've got a guide covering the stolen Chiral Density Gauges locations if you'd like to learn more.

    5. Order 05Defeat the BTs near the former geophysics lab and deliver the Prototype MP bullets
      ObjectiveDefeat BTs west of C1 and deliver prototype bullets to the lab south of where you fight the BTs (southwest of C1).
      TipsThe BTs must be defeated with Blood Grenades, so make sure you bring those along. We also recommend bringing extra Blood Bags and a PCC if you want to build a Timefall Shelter before fighting the BTs. Reaching the lab involves either a longer, but smoother, route or climbing some steep cliffs, so it's best to avoid bringing any heavy cargo with you.
      UnlocksUnlocks Anti-BT Effect for MP Bullets. 300 Likes as base reward, with 570 Additional Likes for content damage 5% or less.
    6. Order 06Defeat the Giant BT
      ObjectiveDefeat the Giant BT.
      TipsThe fight will begin the moment you leave the lab, so you must ensure you're prepared before leaving. We recommend the Assault Rifle for this fight and make some Assault Rifles MP bullets. You'll also want to bring Blood Bags and Blood Grenades.
      Unlocks520 Likes as base reward, with 100 Additional Likes for Sam damage 150% or less.
    7. Order 07Deliver the present for Lou
      ObjectiveReturn to Sam's home and deliver Lou's Present, weighs 1.2kg.
      TipsSince this order requires you to backtrack all the way to where you began the game, it's a good idea to get a vehicle. You'll still have to use it carefully, but it will save a lot of time. We recommend heading to C1 first, so you can restock if needed. It's a good idea to avoid fighting the Ghost Mechs after you've crossed the river.
      UnlocksN/A
